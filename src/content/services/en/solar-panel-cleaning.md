## What solar panel cleaning covers

We clean the surface of the panel glass: dust, pollen, bird droppings and lichen.
The service suits both roof-mounted and ground-mounted arrays.

## How the work is done

A drone does the washing. Nobody steps onto the roof and nobody walks between the
rows of panels — the whole crew stays on the ground. That means nobody stands on
the panels, on their mountings, or on the roof covering.

Before the work we look the site over. We hold an official authorisation to fly
over houses and the work is insured. For housing associations and commercial
clients we supply a copy of the authorisation, proof of liability insurance and a
site-specific risk assessment.

## What solar panel cleaning does not cover

We do not do electrical work. Inspecting, repairing or replacing panels, the
inverter, the cabling and the mountings needs a solar installer. We do not carry
out thermal imaging or output testing either.

If we see cracked glass, a loose mounting or any other fault while cleaning, we
tell you, but we do not fix it ourselves.
