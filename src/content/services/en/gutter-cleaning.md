## What gutter clearing covers

We clear leaves, needles, moss and silt out of the gutters and downpipes, and
check that the water runs through.

A blocked gutter sends water over the edge, against the wall and down beside the
foundation. Preventing that costs less than repairing it later.

## How the work is done

Gutter clearing is booked as an add-on to a roof clean, on the same visit. We do
not currently offer it as a visit of its own, because one short job does not
cover the cost of the trip.

## What gutter clearing does not cover

We do not repair guttering. Loose brackets, gutters running to the wrong fall,
leaking joints and broken pipes need a sheet metal or roofing company. If we see
something like that while working, we tell you, but we do not fix it ourselves.

The service also does not include fitting gutter guards, clearing drainage and
surface water gullies, or removing ice and snow in winter.
