## What window cleaning covers

We clean the outside of windows and glass surfaces where they cannot be reached
from the ground: upper-floor windows, glazed facades, and terrace and balcony
glazing.

The service is meant for exactly those surfaces that would otherwise need
scaffolding, a lift or a rope access team.

## How the work is done

A drone does the washing. The crew stays on the ground, nobody steps onto a sill
or a roof, and no ladder is put against the wall.

Before the work we look the property over: the glass, the height and what
surrounds it. We hold an official authorisation to fly over houses and the work
is insured. For housing associations and commercial clients we supply a copy of
the authorisation, proof of liability insurance and a site-specific risk
assessment.

## What window cleaning does not cover

We do not clean the inside of windows — a drone works outdoors. Interior surfaces
need an ordinary cleaning service.

Cleaning also does not include cleaning frames and sills separately, replacing
seals or glazing, or cleaning blinds and awnings. If we see cracked glass or a
failed seal while cleaning, we tell you, but we do not fix it ourselves.
