## What facade cleaning covers

We remove algae, moss and grime from the wall. The service suits rendered and
painted surfaces, timber cladding and fibre-cement panels.

Pressure and nozzle are chosen to suit the wall material, not the other way
round. Old render and worn paint get low pressure. If a surface cannot take
pressure washing, we say so before the work starts, not after.

## How the work is done

A drone does the washing. The crew stays on the ground and no ladder is put
against the wall. No scaffolding or lift is needed, which means there is no
scaffolding cost and nothing heavy is brought onto your planting.

Before the work we look the property over: the condition of the wall, its height
and what surrounds it. We hold an official authorisation to fly over houses and
the work is insured. For housing associations and commercial clients we supply a
copy of the authorisation, proof of liability insurance and a site-specific risk
assessment.

## What facade cleaning does not cover

We do not repair facades. Cracked render, flaking paint, failed insulation and
broken cladding boards need a building contractor. If we see something like that
while cleaning, we tell you, but we do not fix it ourselves.

Cleaning also does not include painting or rendering the wall, sealing joints, or
cleaning window frames and sills separately. Window cleaning can be booked
together with a facade clean on the same visit.
