## What roof cleaning covers

We remove moss, algae and grime from the roof. The service suits metal, tile and
fibre-cement roofs.

Pressure and nozzle are chosen to suit the roofing material, not the other way
round. Old fibre-cement and worn coatings get low pressure. If a material cannot
take pressure washing, we say so before the work starts, not after.

## How the work is done

A drone does the washing. Nobody steps onto the roof and no ladder is put
against it — the whole crew stays on the ground. No scaffolding or lift is
needed, which means there is no scaffolding cost and nothing heavy is brought
onto your garden.

Before the work we look the property over: the condition of the roof, its pitch
and what surrounds it. We hold an official authorisation to fly over houses and
the work is insured. For housing associations and commercial clients we supply a
copy of the authorisation, proof of liability insurance and a site-specific risk
assessment.

## What roof cleaning does not cover

We do not repair roofs. Cracked tiles, loose flashing and failed penetrations
need a roofing company. If we see something like that while cleaning, we tell
you, but we do not fix it ourselves.

Cleaning also does not include painting or coating the roof, replacing the
guttering, or chimney repair. Clearing gutters and downpipes can be booked
together with a roof clean on the same visit.
